Common questions

Does applying through Study Hood cost anything?

No — submitting your application through us is free. Any fee a specific college charges for its own admission process is separate and set by that college, not by us.

How many colleges can I apply to at once?

List all the colleges you're interested in on the form. We'll confirm which of them we can forward your application to.

How long does it take to hear back?

This depends on each college's own admission timeline. We'll follow up with you as soon as we have an update from them.

What happens to my information?

We use it only to process your application and to contact you about it — see the consent note on the application form for details.
